# Logo Concept

Visual identity for Loop Engineering — feedback closure as brand metaphor.

---

## Concept: The Closed Loop

A circular arrow forming a **closed orbit** with a small gap at the evaluate→observe junction — emphasizing that evaluation closes the loop.

### Elements

1. **Orbit ring** — continuous improvement path
2. **Three nodes** on the ring: Act (A), Observe (O), Evaluate (E)
3. **Center glyph** — minimal τ (termination) mark or check when loop completes
4. **Typography** — "Loop Engineering" in geometric sans (e.g., Inter, IBM Plex Sans)

---

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Loop primary | `#2D6A4F` | Ring, success state |
| Feedback accent | `#457B9D` | Evaluate node, links |
| Terminate | `#1D3557` | Center τ mark |
| Background light | `#F8F9FA` | Docs, slides |
| Background dark | `#0D1117` | IDE, dark mode |

---

## Variants

- **Mark only** — ring + nodes for favicon and GitHub org avatar
- **Wordmark** — mark + "Loop Engineering" horizontal
- **Stacked** — mark above wordmark for slides

---

## Don'ts

- Do not open the ring (broken loop = anti-pattern)
- Do not use infinity ∞ alone (implies unbounded τ)
- Do not imply endorsement by OpenAI, LangGraph, or other frameworks
